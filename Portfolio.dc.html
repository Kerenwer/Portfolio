Electronic Cash Register

The Electronic Cash Register: a smart, Android-based register with a built-in POS.
I led the move from a complex legacy system to a simple, modern one for small, medium and large retailers.

Motivation and pain points

A legacy program
that nobody enjoyed using

  • Too complex to use, with too many clicks for every action
  • Not built for the target audience
  • Poor UX and a dated UI
  • New markets
  • Must fit small, medium and large businesses
  • Must work across several screen sizes
Old design
Research and competitors

Spending time where the register lives

Mapping the old software, analyzing competitors and observing four stores for several days.

  • Mapped every feature of the old software, to see what each action cost the user in clicks and where the clutter came from
  • Analyzed competitors and spent several days observing stores, to understand the pain points in real use
  • Learned about the target audience on site: how small, medium and large businesses work, and which screen sizes they use
  • Asked cashiers and business owners which flows they use most, then prioritized those flows and kept the mandatory actions one tap away
Verifone
POSIT
User interviews

What cashiers and owners told us

  • Too many clicks to change item quantity.
  • Too many buttons.
  • Numbers are used a lot (only in big businesses).
  • Clear screen options are missing, and so is a way to start a new transaction.
  • The details that matter most: name, number, price.
  • Fast payment on the sales screen.
  • Connected/Disconnected
  • After paying with a gift card, if money is left to pay, go straight to the payment screen.

Small devices

Adaptation to small devices

Every design made for the big screen has to work on small handheld devices as well. The same flows, components and rules carry over to a single-column layout, so cashiers get one experience on any device. The design system basics stay the same, with dedicated versions for small, large and XL screens.

In the real world

Behind the counter

AI-generated scenes of the new design in real businesses: a fashion store, a Black Friday counter and a mini-market. They help us and our stakeholders judge scale, readability and flow before anything is installed.
